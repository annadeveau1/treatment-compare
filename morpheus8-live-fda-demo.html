Product nameMorpheus8 — a set of RF microneedling applicators
ManufacturerInMode Ltd.
How they relateMorpheus8 applicators plug into InMode's underlying RF platform. FDA filings list the full device as "InMode System with the Morpheus8 Applicators" — InMode is the company and base platform, Morpheus8 is the specific applicator set.
What this actually means
  • What it is: A device that delivers radiofrequency (RF) energy through tiny needles that penetrate the skin at adjustable depths.
  • What it's FDA-cleared for: Heating and tightening (coagulating/contracting) soft tissue, and controlling minor bleeding during procedures. It's the first RF microneedling device cleared specifically for tightening soft tissue, and separately cleared for full-body fat remodeling.
  • How it works, plainly: The needles deliver heat below the skin's surface. That heat damages fat cells and tightens the deeper skin layer, and the controlled injury is thought to trigger the body's healing response, prompting new collagen production.
  • A real limitation worth asking about: At the highest energy settings (above 62 millijoules per pin), the device is only cleared for lighter to medium skin tones (Fitzpatrick Skin Types I–IV). If you have darker skin, ask your provider whether they're using a setting within that cleared range.
  • Good to know: "Morpheus8" has gone through several separate FDA clearances over the years for different configurations, so the exact hardware and cleared uses can vary by which system a clinic owns.
